1. About REFUGE61

REFUGE61 is a one-week winter experience in the Norwegian mountains, centred around cross-country skiing, nature, simplicity, community and life in a mountain lodge.

The stay runs from Saturday to Saturday.

REFUGE61 is neither a traditional hotel stay nor a classic guided ski holiday with a fixed daily programme. Guests and hosts live together at the lodge, share meals, cook together and spend their days cross-country skiing and enjoying the winter landscape.

Guests are free to choose their own pace and activities. The hosts provide the framework for the week, advise on routes and possibilities, share practical tips on cross-country skiing and join selected outings.The week also includes the opportunity to take part in an optional mini-expedition, with one overnight stay in the winter mountains, subject to weather, snow and safety conditions.”

2. Duration and Accommodation

The specific dates are stated in the individual booking.

The lodge has seven double rooms and accommodates up to 14 guests.

Accommodation is normally based on two guests sharing a room.

Solo travellers booking shared accommodation may be paired with another solo traveller. The applicable room arrangement will be made clear to the guest before the booking becomes binding.

A private room may be booked for an additional charge, subject to availability.

3. What Is Included

The price includes:

accommodation at the lodge

breakfast, lunch and dinner

transport from Vinstra station to the lodge on arrival

transport from the lodge to Vinstra station on departure

nordic cross-country skis

ski boots

ski poles

relevant shared equipment for the mini-expedition

activities during the stay

mini-expedition with one overnight stay

bed linen

towels

bathrobe.

Transport to and from Vinstra is not included.

Guests must bring their own winter and ski clothing, ordinary clothing and footwear, personal medication and snacks.

Beer, wine and other alcoholic beverages are not included. Guests may bring their own alcohol for moderate personal consumption.

4. Arrival and Departure

Guests are responsible for arranging their own transport to and from Vinstra.

Before the trip, REFUGE61 will provide a recommended arrival time at Vinstra station. The shared transfer to the lodge is organised on the basis of this time.

If a guest cannot arrive at the recommended time, alternative arrangements must be agreed with REFUGE61 in advance.

The same principle applies on departure. REFUGE61 will provide a recommended departure time and arrange transport from the lodge to Vinstra station.

Individual arrangements may be possible where practical.

5. Meals and Shared Life

Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included.

REFUGE61 is a shared experience rather than a traditional hotel stay. Preparing food, eating together and taking part in everyday life at the lodge are part of the concept.

Guests are divided into small cooking teams of approximately three people. As a general rule, each team prepares one dinner and one breakfast during the week.

We provide varied food and aim to offer sufficient choice for everyone to eat well.

REFUGE61 does not normally prepare individual meals or separate menus tailored to specific dietary preferences or diets.

Guests must inform REFUGE61 before the stay of any food allergy or dietary requirement that is important for us to know about. Guests with particular dietary needs are encouraged to contact us before booking so that the practical possibilities can be clarified.

11. Cross-Country Skiing and Activities

REFUGE61 does not provide formal cross-country skiing instruction.

The hosts share experience and practical tips, advise on routes and conditions and join selected outings.

Guests decide for themselves which activities and outings they wish to take part in. There is no expectation that everyone follows the same programme or pace.

Previous cross-country skiing experience is not required, but guests should be in normal physical condition and are responsible for assessing whether a chosen activity is appropriate for their own physical and technical ability.

12. Independent Outings and Safety

Guests are generally free to organise their own days and may go on ski outings without the hosts.

For safety reasons, guests going out independently are strongly encouraged to inform the hosts or another person at the lodge of their planned route and expected return time.

Each guest is responsible for choosing an outing appropriate to their own ability and the conditions on the day.

REFUGE61 may recommend or advise against particular routes or activities based on weather, snow conditions, safety or other relevant circumstances.

Cross-country skiing and travel in the winter mountains involve inherent risks. These inherent risks do not in themselves constitute a lack of conformity in the package.

REFUGE61 remains responsible for the performance of the agreed travel services and for injury or loss to the extent required by applicable law.

Nothing in these terms excludes or limits any liability or right that cannot legally be excluded or restricted to the detriment of the guest.

13. The Mini-Expedition

During the week, guests are offered a mini-expedition with one overnight stay in the winter mountains.

Participation is voluntary.

The mini-expedition takes place together with the hosts and is facilitated by REFUGE61. Relevant shared expedition equipment is provided.

The route, timing, overnight location and final format depend on the prevailing weather, temperature, snow and safety conditions.

The hosts may therefore change the route, timing or activity or, where necessary, decide that the mini-expedition cannot take place as originally planned.
